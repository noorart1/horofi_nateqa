Scope: the media player block of the player page (public/index.php `.sc`, its CSS, waveform drawing in player.js). Mode: Operate (a 3–5 year old presses play/next with an adult beside them). Extension of the existing page; the brand world comes from the «حروفي الناطقة» cards and banner.

## Direction contract
THESIS: the letter sings. The current letter sits in the cards' sky-blue ring and bounces to the loudness of its own song; the player is a chunky toy, not a SoundCloud strip with thin grey bars.
OWN-WORLD: sun-yellow field (#ffcb05) from the banner, logo blue (#0070b2) for the play button and played bars, sky ring (#6ed2f2), the cards' red (#f14a26) for the letter glyph; rounded Arabic display face Rubik 800 (Baloo Bhaijaan 2 was tried and dropped: its hamza on «ألف» reads as a fatha, unacceptable in a letter-learning product); fat round buttons with soft offset shadows that press down; pill-shaped waveform bars.
STORY: the child sees their letter, presses the big blue button, watches the letter sing and the blue fill the bars, presses an arrow for the next letter. Grown-up settings stay small at the bottom.
FIRST VIEWPORT: letter badge (~100px) beside the letter name and the writing-practice button; below, a centred row prev | giant play | next (RTL: next on the left); then the fat waveform; then a quiet grown-ups row (shuffle, repeat, speed).
FORM: extension of the existing surface, no concept roll (seed: none).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
