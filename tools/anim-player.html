Singers

0%

A song can have a lead singer and a chorus. Each singer has its own mouth and sings only in its own parts of the song.

Mouth

Drag the white circle onto the mouth and the blue dot to set its width. While singing, a skin-colored patch hides the painted mouth and one of nine shapes is drawn, picked from the sound. The colored band on top of the strip shows the shapes over time.

Click a shape to see it on the picture. "From the sound" goes back to singing.

Click points around the lower jaw or beak. The yellow circle is the hinge the jaw turns on, and the pink circle is the tongue. You can drag all of them.

Put the mouth inside its character's region or a container (▣). It then moves, turns and scales with it and with every container around it, like a region inside a container. A new container you draw around a mouth takes that mouth in by itself.

Voice only

The mouth should move only when the singer sings, not with the music. In the strip under the picture, gray is the whole sound and blue is what moves the mouth; yellow marks the singing parts.

Lead and chorus: "Split lead and chorus" finds which parts are the lead and which are the chorus. "Give it the parts nobody has" is for a chorus singer: it gets every singing part that the other singers do not sing. Each singer's parts show as a thin colored line at the bottom of the strip.

Blinking

Press "Add eye", then drag a box over the eye. The eyelid color is picked from the skin above the eye.

Moving regions

For precise shapes, press "Polygon region" and click points around a character or body part; only that shape moves. The yellow line stays still, and the red cross is the anchor point the shape turns around.

A container moves several regions together. For example, a "body" sways and the "arm" inside it also turns on its own. Put a region in a container with "Inside container".

Picture frame

Only the inside of the frame comes alive; the page header, letter and poem stay still. The frame on book pages is found automatically.

Scene

The heat shimmer runs between the two orange lines, only on sand and sky. Draw an exclusion box over anything that must not shimmer.

The pink circle is the singer’s mouth (bubbles while singing). White dots are bubble sources, and the faint lines are light rays.

The orange circle is the flame glow and the small circle is the engine nozzle. Click to add twinkling stars.

Presets

Pick one, then turn each effect on or off or change its strength.

Effects

Drag the sun, the rainbow's center and size, the rain's ground line and the fog height on the picture. The rainbow and night stars show only in the sky (set the sky band in the Scene tab).

Add an animal

Click an animal, then click on the picture where it lives. On the picture, the white dot moves it and the blue dot sets how far it goes. Pick one in the list below to change its size and speed.

On this page

Camera

While the song plays, the camera goes close to whoever is singing, and pulls back when several sing together or nobody sings. The strip under the picture shows the shots in the singers' colors (gray is the wide shot, white a cutaway, blue marks the amateur's zoom-ins).

Like a parent filming with a phone: shaky hands, a little late to follow, overshoots and corrects, and suddenly zooms into an eye, the nose or the mouth, then pulls back. The blue dots on each singer are their eyes, nose and mouth for these zooms; drag them onto the face.

Cutaways

So the camera does not stay on one singer for long, it looks away for a moment at something else in the picture, at a quiet moment between lines, and comes back.

Shots

Each yellow box is one singer's close-up. Drag its middle to move it and its corner to change the size. Click a name to see that shot.

For the website

Make one file for this letter and upload it in the site's admin panel. The page picture and the song are not inside it (the site already has them); custom mouth sheets and own tap sounds are. Keep these files: they are also your backup, because the browser's own storage can be cleared.
